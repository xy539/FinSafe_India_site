What is FinSafe India?

FinSafe India is a public-awareness initiative that publishes short educational videos in Hindi about common cyber and financial fraud schemes — including UPI scams, phishing, SIM-swap fraud, and investment fraud — targeting everyday Indian internet users.

Our content is distributed primarily through YouTube Shorts and helps viewers identify red flags, protect themselves, and report fraud through official channels such as cybercrime.gov.in and the national helpline 1930.

How We Use Google APIs

YouTube Data API v3 Usage

This application uses the YouTube Data API v3 solely to:

  • Upload educational video content to our own YouTube channel
  • Set video metadata (title, description, tags) on our own uploads
  • Change video visibility (private → public) for our own videos after review

We do not access, read, or modify any data belonging to other users. No user data is collected, stored, or shared. OAuth tokens are stored locally on our own server and are used exclusively for the operations listed above.
